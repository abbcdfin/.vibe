# Agile Method Principles

Read and apply `reference.md` alongside this file. It owns the Agile principles,
method rules and prototype distinctions. The governance Lifecycle owns increment
acceptance, scope and completion; `architectural-principles` owns structure.

Method names:

- Build thin, then widen
- Fail loudly on the unhandled case
- Validate at the edge, assert inside
- Thin and end-to-end over thick and partial
- Spike, thin slice and demo
